Avyakta Portal - Missing Report Website Punjab & Unidentified Persons Portal

Avyakta is India's leading missing report portal and humanitarian database connecting families with unclaimed and unidentified deceased persons since 2020. Our mission is to restore dignity to every unclaimed body and bring closure to grieving families through official police coordination, anonymous reporting, and community support.

How to File a Missing Report Online in Punjab & India

File a report regarding a missing person or an unidentified deceased individual (लावारिस लाश). Submissions can be submitted with mobile OTP verification or completely anonymously. Call our Helpline: +91 62994 46452 for immediate support.
