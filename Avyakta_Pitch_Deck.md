# Slide 1: Title Slide
**Avyakta (Astitva)**
*“A voice for the unspoken. A tribute to the unknown.”*
A secure, socially-driven platform to report and give dignity to unclaimed bodies.

---

# Slide 2: The Problem
**The Silent Crisis**
- **Unreported Cases:** Thousands of deceased individuals remain unidentified and unreported due to fear of police harassment or legal complexities.
- **Lack of Dignity:** The deceased are denied a proper farewell and their families never find closure.
- **System Inefficiency:** Existing reporting mechanisms are manual, tedious, and often deter good Samaritans from coming forward.

---

# Slide 3: The Solution
**Avyakta: Empathy meets Technology**
Avyakta is a digital platform that bridges the gap between citizens and authorities. 
- **Fearless Reporting:** Citizens can report incidents completely anonymously.
- **Verified Leads:** OTP-verified reporting for those willing to share their details.
- **Seamless Action:** A dedicated Admin panel for police and NGOs to act swiftly.

---

# Slide 4: Key Features
**How It Works**
1. **Anonymous & OTP Modes:** Flexibility to report without sharing identity, or with Twilio OTP verification.
2. **Geo-Location Integration:** Auto-captures precise location to help authorities reach the spot immediately.
3. **Emergency SOS:** 1-tap floating button for Police (100), Ambulance (108), Fire (101).
4. **Bilingual UI:** English and Hindi support for broader accessibility across India.

---

# Slide 5: Target Audience & Social Impact
**Who We Serve**
- **Citizens:** Empowering the common man to act without fear.
- **Authorities (Police/Hospitals):** Providing them with accurate, timely, and organized data.
- **NGOs:** Helping social organizations track and manage proper final rites.
**The Impact:** Restoring dignity to the deceased, bringing closure to families, and fostering a responsible society.

---

# Slide 6: Our Technology
**Built for Speed and Security**
- **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS (Responsive & Bilingual).
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js.
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas (Secure & Scalable).
- **Security:** JWT Authentication, bcrypt encryption for admin data, IP logging for audit trails.
- **APIs:** Twilio for OTP, Geolocation APIs.
